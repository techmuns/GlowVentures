A Family Office Operating System (FOOS)

It is meant to compound knowledge, decisions and capital over decades. The system should answer 5 questions instantly:

What do we own?Portfolio Monitor, consolidated to ₹335.43 Cr with each dually-reported holding counted once
Why do we own it?Thesis & Triggers records why, expected return, risk and exit triggers per holding
What has changed?Morning CIO, news and corporate actions cover it; there is no automated thesis-change monitor
What opportunities exist?Watchlist, company compare, the macro store and a market-cap view of the book; still no screener ACROSS companies
What should we do next?Alerts fire on the family's own rules, and the Decisions Required queue is built on the Family Dashboard
