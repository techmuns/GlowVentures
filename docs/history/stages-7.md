### Stage 10oo — Statement Library: a spreadsheet source opens on the cited row and cell

- *"it should have opened the pdf right then why did it open this kind of table ?"* — the family, 16 Nov 2026, after clicking Ajay's 1,77,000 Vedanta Aluminium units under HDFC Securities 3110320 on the Portfolio Monitor. The link was right: HDFC Securities sends that account's holdings only as a CSV (the PDF in the same ZIP is ICICI's NSDL 49794950). What was missing was the pointer. A PDF opens on the cited page with the number marked; a spreadsheet marked a row only when the link carried `rows=`, and a statement record's link carries `record=` instead.
- `src/lib/sheetSourceCell.ts` finds the cited record inside its own file: the one row printing the record's own ISIN, printed name or symbol, narrowed by the exact figure, and the one cell carrying it. When the figure appears twice in that row, the column heading for the link's field decides (QTY, not the identical LONG TERM QTY). A link without a figure (market value, cost, price) uses the record's own figure for that field. With anything short of one row it abstains and says so, as the PDF view does. Matching on an equal amount alone is never evidence.
- `StatementPreview` reads the record (`document.json` at the pointer) only for a spreadsheet link with `record=`. It marks the row (champagne tint) and the cell (the PDF view's yellow highlighter, `td[data-source-cell]` in `index.css`) in both Original file and Text as read, and scrolls sideways only as far as the cell needs, so the row's name stays in view. Review-workbook links (`rows=`) are unchanged.
- Measured: 6 of the book's 8 spreadsheet-sourced holdings now land on their units' cell. The other 2 (STL Networks, Sterlite Technologies under 3110320) come from the depository-view CSV, while their link opens the equity CSV, which does not list them. They show "could not be uniquely highlighted" rather than marking a wrong row. `src/lib/__tests__/sheetSourceCell.test.ts`.
