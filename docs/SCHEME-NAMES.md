scheme and ETF this book holds, joined to the book **by ISIN** through
`public/lookthrough/` and therefore through the AMC's own filing — or, for a
scheme that store has never seen, through AMFI's own daily file on the ISIN the
family's statement prints (`src/data/fundNavs.ts`; joined by `amfi-isin` below).
Nothing here is matched on a resemblance, and nothing reaches `glowData.ts`: it is
the label a reader sees, and `securityKeyOf` is not routed through it.
